# Dressmaker — Élan Atelier

`8novclthingsites.zip` contains thirteen Wix design-reference screenshots, not source code or executable instructions. The reference is a dressmaker/bridal atelier, rather than a general clothing checkout. This release adds one original JKTL template, `dressmaker-atelier`, to both catalogues (209 total, 207 designed plus two blanks). No Wix assets, names, testimonials or newsletter backend are copied.

The design has a full-bleed bridal hero, cream editorial sections, a four-look collection with accessible native detail dialogs, a three-step consultation journey, craft photography, FAQs, service areas and contact enquiry form. Desktop navigation becomes a mobile menu; a JKTL Business login icon stays available. Collection hover uses Framer Motion and respects reduced motion. The public gallery keeps free preview/new-tab viewing and the existing selection/sign-up flow; Business keeps its multi-site creation flow.

The standard editor changes the copy, brand/logo, colours, photos, gallery, steps, FAQs, contact text and areas. Collection photo and price/enquiry-label fields are exposed for this design. Example collection names describe design inspiration; actual quotes and availability are agreed with the business. No product checkout, stock availability or booking confirmation is invented.

The consultation form validates name/email/message, then opens the visitor’s email app to the published business’s configured contact address. It tells the visitor to send from their email app. It does not claim to store or deliver an enquiry itself. Preview submissions only show an explanatory message; no email, booking or customer record is created. Missing contact details are stated plainly. Reviews are omitted until actual organization testimonials exist, or the business adds genuine reviews and disables the sample-content flag. Socials render only configured HTTPS links.

Nine reviewed Pexels assets are bundled locally under `public/template-photos/african/atelier-*.jpg`. Source descriptions, search context, links and licence are in `sources.json`; human photos follow the existing African/Black fashion-photography direction. These are stock demonstration images, not real clients of the fictional atelier. Runtime image optimization is avoided for these compressed assets. A local SVG is the failure fallback; gallery previews use an actual rendered screenshot.

Validation: shared catalogue/schema/renderer tests, paired repository parity, local production builds, and `tests/dressmaker-template.browser.cjs` cover responsive navigation, collection-dialog keyboard behavior, preview enquiry notice, no duplicate H1, working photos and no horizontal overflow. Existing saved websites and databases are unchanged. All changes stay local until JKTL explicitly requests deployment.
