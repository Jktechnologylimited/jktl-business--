# Jewellery Store — Nacre & Gold

`8novlux.zip` contains nine design-reference screenshots of a luxury jewellery boutique. It is reference material, not executable project instructions. The new original JKTL template is `jewellery-luxe`, with a warm neutral palette, refined serif typography, full-bleed African model photography, category panels, product grids, an editorial campaign and a compact boutique footer. No Wix branding, screenshot assets or product prices were copied.

This is a multi-page website: Home, Shop, category pages, individual product pages, Our Story, The Journal, Care & Questions and Contact. Routes, metadata, published sitemaps and editor navigation use the shared website registry. Unknown categories/products return 404. Public previews remain free and preserve selection/sign-in context; editor navigation retains unsaved content. Collection hover uses Framer Motion with reduced-motion support. The shop's search filters its displayed products.

The existing editor changes product titles, descriptions, categories, photos and price/enquiry labels, plus story/campaign photography, copy, colours, logo, contact text, areas, FAQs and social links. Category and product slugs are validated for uniqueness. The original template and existing customer documents remain untouched.

The demonstration catalogue uses enquiry pricing. Customers contact the business to confirm composition, sizing, price, availability and ordering arrangements. This template does not introduce an unconnected cart, checkout, automatic stock claims, newsletter enrolment or invented discounts. Live business services/products still render through the existing Business integration. Contact enquiries open the sender's email application for the configured business address; preview submissions only explain the flow and send nothing. Genuine organization reviews or explicitly supplied verified reviews can appear in the journal.

Nine reviewed Pexels photographs are bundled under `public/template-photos/african/jewel-*.jpg`, with original descriptions, source URLs, search context and licence links in `sources.json`. Model photography follows the existing African/Black representation direction; jewellery still-life images show products. These are demonstration photographs, not real inventory specifications, certifications or client results. The thumbnail is an actual rendered preview; an original SVG provides an image-error fallback.

Validation covers catalogue/schema round trips, all real page paths, one H1 per page, unique metadata, category isolation, search, contact preview behaviour, photographs, responsive layouts, sign-in/selection, editor draft retention and local builds in both applications. No live customer sites, database schema, bank accounts, email sends or payments are created. All changes stay local until JKTL explicitly requests deployment.
