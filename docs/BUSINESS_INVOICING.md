# Business invoice delivery and settlement

Business owners add a receiving bank under **Settings → Payments**, verify the account name and save. The server resolves the bank/account again, creates or updates the existing Paystack subaccount, and stores its confirmed code. Only active owners may change bank details; owners/managers can create and send invoices. Existing invoices remain intact. Both the server action and invoice INSERT require a receiving account; live invoice creation waits for server confirmation instead of showing a successful offline draft that cannot be paid.

Invoices and receipts use `Business Name via JKTL Business <notifications@mail.jktl.com.ng>`. The display name comes from the authenticated business profile and is header-sanitized. The mailbox stays fixed on the previously verified JKTL sending domain. Shared branding, provider, promotional preferences and administrator-configured Reply-To remain unchanged; no mailbox is created per business.

The existing Paystack hosted checkout splits the invoice amount to the saved business subaccount and the existing ₦50 platform fee to JKTL. No customer wallet or new fee is introduced. Payment confirmation is distinct from bank settlement: Paystack controls settlement timing. Signed webhooks must match invoice organization, exact NGN total including the existing fee and successful payment status before marking paid. Duplicate paid events do not send a second receipt. Business owners receive an email and existing PWA notifications, and customers receive the receipt. Manual marking-paid sends notices without claiming an automatic bank transfer happened.

Provider sends are awaited and best-effort; this release does not promise mailbox delivery or introduce a durable notification queue. Automated tests use fake bank, checkout and email responses. No real bank details, subaccounts, customer invoice emails, payments or settlements are created while testing. Live end-to-end settlement requires the deployed Paystack secret/webhook settings and an authorized payment to a real verified receiving account.
