# Age-well template direction

Primary industries, in priority order: Beauty & Aesthetics; Senior Care; Wealth &
Retirement; Cosmetic Dentistry; Men's Health & Wellness; Health & Longevity.
The four pillars are Beauty & Youth, Health & Longevity, Wealth & Protection,
and Vitality & Intimacy. Children's and financial education remain secondary.

The immediate template sequence is medical spas, senior home care and wealth
advisors. Keep the three existing offers clear in platform distribution: free
websites, free professional invoicing and easy online payments. Existing payment
fees and product eligibility remain unchanged.

The owner then supplied `med.zip` and `med 1.zip` as the first visual references.
`med.zip` contains a 15-second screen recording, not website source code. Its
cream, serif-led medical-spa layout informs the original **Medical Spa — Nacre**
template (`nacre-aesthetics`). The original `med 1.zip` exceeded the workspace's
32 MiB transfer limit. Two subsequent readable recordings, dated 15 November at
18:27:02 and 18:27:31, now provide the additional Lumivie and Luneform references.
Senior-care/wealth templates remain next in the wider strategy, rather than
being silently counted as completed.

Nacre is mirrored in Main and Business using the existing catalogue, JSON editor,
published-site renderer, request handling, metadata, sitemap and template signup
paths. It has 13 routes: home, about, treatments, three individual treatments,
gallery, appointment request, contact and four care questions. Selection remains
separate from public preview access; no signup is required to explore it.

**Medical Spa — Lumivie** (`lumivie-skin`) adds a forest-green/cream editorial
design, rectangular treatment cards, a practitioner-approach section and four
photographic care steps. **Aesthetic Clinic — Luneform** (`luneform-aesthetics`)
uses warm full-width photography, translucent controls and a four-step journey.
Its buttons update the step text and photograph; Tab/Enter work, selected state
is announced and no autoplay/polling is introduced. Each has its own catalogue
entry, photograph selection, headings, service copy and 13 pages. They share the
existing CMS/request infrastructure, with validated `medicalVariant` values.
No reference-site financing, subscription, certification, review-count or
free-consultation claim is imported into the sample business.

Its Framer Motion heading reveal is bounded to one viewport entry and respects
reduced motion. Content remains server-rendered and visible. Mobile navigation,
native FAQ disclosures, visible focus styles and a skip link support accessibility.
The contact/appointment forms reuse the existing authorized public enquiry flow;
preview forms explicitly report that no request was sent. A request is never
presented as a confirmed appointment. No patient system or payment module is added.

Photographs reuse the local Pexels African collection with existing provenance
in `public/template-photos/african/sources.json`. They are editorial illustrations,
not patient outcomes. No fabricated reviews, prices, clinician credentials, staff
profiles, clinical guarantees or years-in-practice claims are seeded. Contact
details start empty and are supplied by the business.

Development is local; deployment requires a new explicit owner instruction.
